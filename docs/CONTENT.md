## 4. Brand Voice

The writing should be:

-   Direct
-   Professional
-   Operational
-   Confident
-   Concise
-   B2B-oriented

Avoid:

-   Empty corporate buzzwords
-   Unverified superlatives
-   Fake urgency
-   Overpromising
-   Unsupported statistics

## 5. Claims Policy

Do not publish claims such as:

-   "Industry leader"
-   "No. 1"
-   "20+ years of experience"
-   "500+ projects"
-   "1000+ clients"
-   "24/7 support"
-   "100% satisfaction"

unless the business supplies evidence and explicitly approves them.

## 6. Testimonials

Do not create placeholder testimonials that look real.

If testimonials are unavailable:

-   Hide the section, or
-   Use a clearly marked internal placeholder during development.

## 7. Project Content

Projects must be sourced from the business.

Required approval before publishing:

-   Project name
-   Client/company name
-   Location
-   Images
-   Description
-   Permission to publish

## 8. Fleet Content

Fleet/equipment pages should use actual inventory.

Required data:

-   Equipment name
-   Category
-   Image
-   Availability
-   Specifications
-   Rental/transport terms if publicly publishable
